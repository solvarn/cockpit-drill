Cockpit Drill Octavia III, manual

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger cockpit.

Pedals: the % is how far the pedal is pushed in (clutch 100% = fully pressed, engine disconnected). Press and slide down to push the pedal in (it moves away from you), slide up to let it come back. A pedal stays where you leave it, so you can hold the clutch down and still use the key. The thin bar beside each pedal follows your finger and shows how far it is in.
